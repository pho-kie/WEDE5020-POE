About us

Our salon was established in early 2019. It started as a very small organization with very little services to provide for our customers which was braiding all types of hair and haircuts. Our main goal was to create an environment for our small community to get their desires to live. We unfortunately had to pause on our services during COVID-19. However, right after the pandemic was over, we managed to get back on our feet, and we became better than before. Our services have become polished, better and effective during the COVID-19 period, and we are prepared to provide for our customer's needs and wants.


Our Mission

As an organization we aim to give what our customers need and to do every hairstyle, haircut, nails, and tattoos to the best of our capability and to make every each customer feel confident and cared for.
